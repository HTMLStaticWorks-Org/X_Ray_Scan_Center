Referral Guide

How to Select the Right Examination

Selecting diagnostic scans is straightforward when following your physician's clinical advice.

01
Step 01 • Prescription

Check Doctor's Referral

Locate the exact examination name, body part, and view (e.g., Chest PA, USG Abdomen) on your prescription.

02
Step 02 • Protocol

Confirm Preparation

Check if fasting or bladder filling is necessary for your scan using our Patient Preparation Guide.

03
Step 03 • Scheduling

Select Date & Slot

Choose an available morning or afternoon appointment slot that accommodates your fasting schedule.

04
Step 04 • Arrival

Fast-Track Check-In

Arrive with your referral slip and ID for zero-wait registration and immediate scan room entry.
