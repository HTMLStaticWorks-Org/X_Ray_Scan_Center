Acoustic Sonography Suite

Ultrasound & Color Doppler Imaging

Non-invasive, high-definition acoustic visualization with real-time hemodynamic flow mapping, 3D/4D volumetric surface rendering, and experienced consultant sonologists.

Home / Services / Ultrasound & Sonography
Non-Ionizing Precision

Zero Radiation. Superior Soft-Tissue Resolution.

Ultrasound utilizes high-frequency acoustic sound waves (2 to 18 MHz) rather than ionizing radiation, making it the safest imaging modality for obstetrics, pediatrics, and recurrent monitoring.

Equipped with single-crystal active matrix transducers and speckle-reduction filtering, our system provides distinct border delineation for hepatobiliary anatomy, kidney parenchyma, pelvic organs, and vascular lumen velocity.

Scan Protocol

The Ultrasound Appointment Process

What to expect from appointment arrival to receiving your finalized sonologist evaluation.

1

Preparation Verification

Our nursing staff verifies fasting status (for abdominal scans) or ensures adequate bladder filling (for pelvic scans).

2

Acoustic Gel Application

You recline comfortably on the examination couch. A warm, water-soluble hypoallergenic gel is applied to the skin.

3

Real-Time Probe Scanning

The consultant radiologist gently guides the transducer across the area, capturing dynamic biometry and Doppler audio.

4

Instant Results Discussion

Clean up with warm towels. The radiologist explains immediate key findings and hands over your verified report and photos.

Probe Technology

Advanced Ultrasound Probes for Every Scan

Multi-frequency single-crystal transducers calibrated for maximum tissue penetration and micro-vascular flow detection.

2.0 - 5.0 MHz

Curvilinear Abdominal Probe

Wide-angle deep-penetration acoustic lens designed for hepatic parenchyma, gallbladder, pancreatic, and renal morphology evaluations.

7.5 - 14.0 MHz

High-Frequency Linear Probe

Sub-millimeter superficial resolution probe optimizing thyroid nodule elastography, scrotal sonography, and musculoskeletal tendon tears.

3.5 - 8.0 MHz

Phased Array Cardiac Doppler

Fast-acquisition acoustic matrix designed for intercostal acoustic windows, directional color Doppler velocities, and cardiac chamber motion.

5.0 - 9.0 MHz

Endovaginal Micro-Curved Probe

High-definition internal pelvic acoustic transducer capturing endometrial stripe thickness, ovarian follicles, and early pregnancy sacs.

Obstetric Care

Trimester-by-Trimester Sonography Timeline

Essential gestational sonography milestones guiding maternal and fetal health throughout pregnancy.

6 - 9 Weeks

Early Pregnancy Viability

Confirms intrauterine gestational sac implantation, embryonic cardiac flicker, and precise crown-rump length (CRL) dating.

11 - 13.6 Weeks

NT & Aneuploidy Scan

Nuchal translucency measurement and nasal bone assessment for early chromosomal screening and gross anatomical structural development.

18 - 22 Weeks

Detailed Anomaly (TIFFA)

Exhaustive head-to-toe organ survey evaluating fetal brain ventricles, cardiac four-chamber view, spine, kidneys, and extremities.

28 - 36 Weeks

Growth & Umbilical Doppler

Third-trimester fetal weight estimation, amniotic fluid index (AFI), and placental arterial flow velocity for safe delivery planning.
