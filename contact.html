Appointment & Visiting FAQs

Common questions regarding appointment scheduling, walk-ins, and companion rules.

Yes, routine digital X-rays and resting ECG tests accept walk-in patients during all working hours. However, pre-booking ensures prioritized zero-wait entry.
Because ultrasound examinations require dedicated doctor time and pre-scan fasting or bladder preparation, booking an advance time slot is strongly recommended.
Upon submitting our booking form, you will receive an immediate on-screen reference code followed by an SMS and WhatsApp confirmation with fasting instructions.
For ultrasound, one family attendant may accompany obstetric patients. For X-ray, companions remain in our waiting lounge unless a minor child requires parent reassurance with a protective lead apron.
