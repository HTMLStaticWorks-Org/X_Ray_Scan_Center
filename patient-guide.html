Patient Guidance & Protocol

Patient Preparation Guide

Ensure accurate diagnostic findings by following these straightforward pre-scan preparation guidelines. Clear instructions for fasting, clothing, medication, and required documents.

Home / Patient Guide
Scan Requirements

How to Prepare for Your Scan

Find specific instructions for the examination prescribed by your clinician.

Digital X-Ray Preparation

No dietary restrictions or fasting needed. Wear comfortable cotton clothing without metallic zippers, hooks, or metallic sequins.

  • Remove necklaces, piercings, and hairpins
  • Notify staff if pregnancy is suspected
  • Comfortable changing gowns provided

Ultrasound Protocols

Preparation depends on the anatomical region being scanned (upper abdomen vs. pelvic structures).

  • Upper Abdomen: 4-6 hours fasting (water permitted)
  • Pelvis / Obstetric: Drink 1L water 1 hour prior (full bladder)
  • Thyroid / Vascular: No preparation required

12-Lead ECG Preparation

Completely non-invasive test. Wear a loose two-piece garment so your chest, wrists, and ankles can be accessed easily.

  • Avoid applying body lotions or oils
  • Refrain from caffeine or cigarettes 2 hours prior
  • Rest quietly 5 minutes before recording

Specialized MRI & CT Scans

For contrast-enhanced scans, a recent Serum Creatinine test (within 30 days) is required to verify baseline renal clearance.

  • 4 hours fasting before contrast administration
  • Declare pacemakers, cochlear implants, or stents
  • Stay well-hydrated post examination
Appointment Checklist

What to Bring on Appointment Day

Having these essential items ready expedites your reception check-in and ensures clinical safety.

Doctor's Referral Letter

Bring the original signed medical prescription specifying the required scan, anatomical orientation, and relevant clinical indications.

Previous Diagnostic Films

Bring any previous X-rays, ultrasound scans, CT/MRI discs, or blood reports. Comparison with historical baseline is vital for radiologists.

Government Photo ID

A valid government photo identity proof (Aadhaar, Passport, Driver's License) is required for patient verification and PCPNDT statutory records.

Current Medications List

A list of routine medications you are currently taking, particularly blood thinners, diabetic therapy (Metformin), or cardiac medications.

Patient Journey

Day-of-Visit Protocol

Tips for a smooth, stress-free diagnostic appointment.

1

Arrive 15 Minutes Early

Allows sufficient time for registration, verification of referral papers, and changing into examination clothing.

2

Wear Metal-Free Attire

Loose-fitting two-piece cotton clothes are ideal. Secure lockers are provided for your jewelry and valuables.

3

Disclose Health History

Inform your technologist about pregnancy, metal implants, surgical clips, allergies, or recent surgeries prior to your scan.

4

Attendant & Chaperone

One family member may accompany you to the waiting lounge. Female chaperones are present during all female sonography exams.
