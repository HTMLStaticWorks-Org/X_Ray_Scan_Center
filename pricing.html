Billing Integrity

Honest, All-Inclusive Pricing Promise

Every tariff is standardized, published openly, and honored with clinical transparency.

Pillar 01

Zero Consumable Markups

We never charge extra for diagnostic films, disposable ECG suction cups, acoustic coupling gel, or sanitized patient gowns. The published fee is complete.

Pillar 02

Inclusive Radiologist Reading

Every fee includes formal double-reading and verification by MD / DNB radiologists. You are never billed a separate "radiologist consultation" surcharge.

Pillar 03

Permanent DICOM Cloud Access

Digital image slices, measurement tools, and downloadable high-resolution DICOM records remain permanently available for your physicians at zero extra cost.

Pillar 04

Stat Emergency Price Lock

Even for emergency trauma or urgent same-day walk-in diagnostic investigations, baseline examination fees remain strictly identical to scheduled appointments.
